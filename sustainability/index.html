Sustainability

Billboard is proud of our commitment to providing responsibly sourced and sustainably manufactured offerings that contribute to a better world. Utilizing materials such as recycled plastic, organic cotton, bamboo, reclaimed wood, etc., as well as creating items intended for multi-use, we are always looking for ways to close the consumption loop.

This is a part of our mission to lowering the carbon footprint and making a positive impact on our ecosystems. We have developed long successful partnerships with manufactures who share our commitment to upholding the safety and well-being of those individuals who produce our products, while using innovative manufacturing processes and materials that are recycled and eco-friendly.
